01 — ABOUT

A platform for curiosity, collaboration and discovery.

The Chemical Frontiers Consortium is an academic and scientific initiative dedicated to advancing chemistry through education, research, scientific discussion and open exchange of knowledge.

OUR VISION

Connecting classroom chemistry with the frontiers of research.

CFC seeks to create a collaborative environment where ideas can be discussed, knowledge can be shared, and emerging areas of chemistry can be explored through lectures, research discussions, workshops, educational resources and collaborative projects.

Scientific Understanding

Promoting deeper understanding of chemical principles rather than rote memorisation.

Open Exchange

Encouraging the sharing of ideas, resources, methods and scientific perspectives.

Interdisciplinary Thinking

Exploring chemistry alongside physics, biology, mathematics, computation and materials science.
