Organisational roles

These are possible positions for future contributors. Roles should correspond to genuine responsibilities and active participation.

FounderEstablishes the consortium and defines its founding vision and objectives.
Co-FounderAssists in establishing and developing the consortium.
PresidentLeads the consortium and makes strategic decisions.
ChairpersonOversees governance and major activities.
Executive DirectorManages day-to-day operations and administration.
Secretary-GeneralCoordinates members, programmes and consortium activities.
Vice PresidentSupports the President and oversees assigned responsibilities.
DirectorLeads specific programmes, departments or initiatives.
Research DirectorCoordinates research-oriented programmes and collaborations.
Academic DirectorOversees academic programmes and educational initiatives.
Communications DirectorManages communication, announcements and public outreach.
Technical DirectorOversees the website, digital infrastructure and technical resources.
Editorial DirectorCoordinates articles, publications, educational material and newsletters.
Programme CoordinatorOrganises specific programmes, workshops and events.
Outreach CoordinatorDevelops connections with students, researchers and institutions.
Student RepresentativeRepresents student members and coordinates student activities.
