03 — AREAS OF ACTIVITY

From fundamental concepts to chemical frontiers.

CFC can grow across multiple areas of chemistry while keeping collaboration and scientific understanding at its centre.

01 — Physical ChemistryThermodynamics, kinetics, quantum chemistry, statistical mechanics, spectroscopy and electrochemistry.
02 — Organic ChemistryOrganic reactions, mechanisms, spectroscopy, synthesis and modern developments in organic chemistry.
03 — Inorganic ChemistryCoordination chemistry, organometallic chemistry, bioinorganic chemistry and solid-state chemistry.
04 — Computational & Theoretical ChemistryQuantum chemistry, molecular dynamics, computational spectroscopy, modelling and scientific programming.
05 — Materials & Molecular ScienceMolecular materials, nanoscience, molecular magnetism, functional materials and emerging technologies.
06 — Chemical EducationConceptual lectures, problem solving, educational resources and academic learning initiatives.
