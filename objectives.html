02 — OBJECTIVES

What we aim to build.

Creating an environment where learning, research and scientific exchange reinforce one another.

01

Advance Chemistry Education

Develop accessible, concept-oriented resources covering fundamental and advanced areas of chemistry.

02

Promote Research

Encourage research discussions, scientific exchange and collaborative initiatives in contemporary chemistry.

03

Connect People

Bring students, researchers and educators together to exchange ideas and develop collaborations.

04

Encourage Interdisciplinary Chemistry

Explore connections between chemistry, physics, biology, mathematics, computation and emerging technologies.

05

Build Open Resources

Create and share lectures, notes, problem sets, computational resources, articles and useful academic material.

06

Support Young Researchers

Provide opportunities for students and early-career researchers to present ideas, discuss research and collaborate.
